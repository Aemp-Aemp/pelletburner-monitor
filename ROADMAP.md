# Roadmap

Ideas and planned work. Nothing here is promised; suggestions are welcome.

## Bring the best diagnostic charts from the classic page to the home page

The classic page (`/classic`) has charts that are very good for finding problems, but it is technical and
not for everyday use. The home page (`/home`) is the friendly page. Some of the classic charts should move to
the home page, in a simpler form, so that ordinary users can see what is going on without the classic page.

Candidates (from the classic tabs):

- **Step by step** - how long each part of a start takes (lighting, heating up), compared with normal.
  Shows a dirty burner plate, a weak igniter or poor pellets early.
- **History & tips** - boiler, flue and flame over time with the burner's states underneath.
- **Discoveries** - the cards built from the history: pellets per plan period, time at each power level,
  cooling compared with the outside temperature, "was it warm in time?", lighting time per pellet batch.

How:

- Get a UI/UX design review first (a designer, or a design agent) so the home page stays calm and simple:
  which charts earn a place, where they go (e.g. a "Health" or "Why?" section), and how they look on a phone.
- Plain words, English and Danish, no technical field names.
- Keep the classic page for the technical view.

## Other

- Easy setup for non-technical users: a ready card image for the Raspberry Pi.
- Wi-Fi setup without a PC.
